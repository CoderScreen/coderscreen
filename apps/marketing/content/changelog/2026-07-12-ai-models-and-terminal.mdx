- The AI chat in interview rooms now offers Claude Haiku 4.5, Claude Sonnet 4.5, GPT-5 Mini and GPT-5, replacing older models that were being retired. GPT-5 Mini is the new default.
- If the shell in a room's terminal exits (say someone types `exit`), the terminal starts a fresh session instead of getting stuck reconnecting.
