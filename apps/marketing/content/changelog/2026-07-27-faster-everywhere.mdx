---
title: 'Faster pages and assessment editing'
date: '2026-07-27'
summary: 'Database work across the app got quicker, and building an assessment no longer reloads everything on each edit.'
tags: ['Improved']
---

A few customers told us CoderScreen felt slow when building tests. We went after the causes.

- Database queries now go through a connection pool close to our servers, which cuts time off nearly every request.
- Added missing indexes and removed repeated queries behind the assessment, submission and invite pages.
- Adding, editing or reordering test cases updates the page in place instead of refetching the whole assessment.
